About: These are model simulations of snowpack features and avalanche hazard. The simulations on any particular day have not been checked for accuracy or verified by humans. Model predictions should always be interpreted with caution and verified through field observations and expert assessment. Simulations use a SNOWPACK-style multilayer snow model driven by Parks Canada FTS weather stations (via the Rockies Weather Data Explorer) and Environment and Climate Change Canada HRDPS/RDPS forecasts (via Parks Wx Fx).
